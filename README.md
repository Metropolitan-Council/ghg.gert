The `{ghg.ccap}` repository contains data, code, and documentation for the Twin Cities Metropolitan Statistical Area's Comprehensive Climate Action Plan (CCAP) of the EPA's Climate Pollution Reduction Grant.
The CCAP will expand on previous Met Council Greenhouse Gas (GHG) inventories, ghg.cprg, ghg.sp, in several important ways.

1) expands to four additional collar counties. 
2) quantify agricultural sector emissions and natural land carbon sequestration.
3) develop a baseline year in line with the state of MN's efforts, 2005.
4) project GHG emission out to 2030 and 2050.
5) identifies additional emission reduction measures and quantifies there short and long-term emission reduction potential.
6) quantifies co-benefits to low income and disadvantaged communities as defined by the EPA

This repository was originally forked from ghg.sp and has the potential to ultimately update the strategy planing tool based on the expanded inventory items of the CCAP.
